Real talk

What a cash offer really is

Most cash buyers dance around this. We would rather you hear it from us on the front end than figure it out later.

A cash offer is below retail. It has to be. You are trading some price for speed, certainty, zero cost, and zero work. That is the whole deal, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something.

So here is our rule. If your house is in decent shape, you can wait four to six months, and you can afford to front the repairs out of pocket, list it with an agent. You will probably net more. We will tell you that on the phone, for free, before you ever sign anything.

But if the house needs work you cannot fund, if there is a probate or a foreclosure clock running, if it is full of a lifetime of belongings, or if you simply want this handled and behind you by the end of the month, that is exactly where we are the better answer.

Either way, we will walk you through how we got to your number and show you the comps we used. No black box, no pressure, no games.

Probate is not finished yet. Can I still sell?

Usually yes. In Florida, once the estate is opened and a personal representative is appointed, you can sign a contract and open escrow before probate closes. We just cannot fund until the personal representative has the court's authority. So we start now and close when you are clear.

I cannot afford to start probate.

Most people can't, and that is exactly why these houses sit for years. Here is the part nobody tells you: many South Florida probate attorneys will take an estate with a sale in motion and get paid at closing out of the proceeds. Nothing out of your pocket. We have our own attorney and you should have your own, since we sit on opposite sides of the table. If you do not have one, ask and we will hand you a few names to choose from.

My brothers and sisters cannot agree.

A house is impossible to split. Money is easy. A clean cash sale turns the thing nobody can divide into something everyone can, at a number and a date everybody sees in writing at the same time. We have walked a lot of families through exactly this.

I live out of state and it is full of her things.

Leave every bit of it. Furniture, clothes, paperwork, the garage, all of it. Take what matters to you and walk away from the rest. We handle the cleanout. The entire sale can be done by phone, email and remote notary. You never have to fly down.

Straight answers

Questions people actually ask

How fast can you really close?

Seven days is realistic when title is clean and you are ready. Fourteen days is typical. If there is a probate, a lien, an open permit, or a title issue, it takes longer, and we will tell you that honestly up front instead of promising a week and missing it. You can also go slower on purpose. If you need 60 days to find your next place, that is completely fine.

Are there any fees, commissions, or costs to me?

No. Zero commissions, zero fees, and we pay the standard closing costs. The number on your offer is the number that gets wired to you, minus only what you already owe on the property, like a mortgage payoff, back taxes, or liens. There is nothing buried in the paperwork.

Will you lowball me?

We are not going to pretend a cash offer is retail. It is not, and we say so right on this page. What we will not do is waste your time with a number that does not make sense, and we will not quote you high to get the contract and then grind you down after inspection. If we cannot pay enough to actually help you, we will say so on the first call and point you somewhere better. We are just a totally different company than the rest.

Do I need to clean it out or fix anything?

Nothing. Not one thing. Do not paint, do not repair, do not haul anything to the dump, do not even sweep. Take the belongings that matter to you and leave the rest exactly where it is. We handle the cleanout.

Can I sell a house that is still in probate?

Absolutely. Once the estate is opened with the Florida circuit court and a personal representative has been appointed, you can accept an offer and sign a contract. The sale simply funds once the personal representative has the court's authority to convey. We do this constantly and we know the process cold.

We have our own attorney, and you should have your own. We are on opposite sides of the table, so you want somebody looking out for you and only you. If you do not have one, ask us and we will point you toward a few reputable South Florida probate attorneys and let you pick.

What if the heirs do not agree on selling?

It is one of the most common reasons these homes sit empty for years. A house cannot be split; cash can. Everyone sees the same number and the same closing date in writing at the same time, which takes the argument off the table. In a formal administration, the personal representative generally has authority to sell with court approval. We can walk you through what applies to your estate.

How do you come up with the offer?

We look at what the house would realistically sell for once it is fixed up, what it will cost to get it there, and what it costs us to carry and resell it. Then we work backward to a number that is fair to you and still works for us. We will walk you through every piece of it on the phone and show you the comps we used, so you can check our math instead of taking our word for it.
