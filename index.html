Probate is not finished yet. Can I still sell?

Usually yes. In Florida, once the estate is opened and a personal representative is appointed, you can sign a contract and open escrow before probate closes. We just cannot fund until the personal representative has the court's authority. So we start now and close when you are clear.

I cannot afford to start probate.

Most people can't, and that is exactly why these houses sit for years. We will connect you with a South Florida probate attorney at no upfront cost to you. They get paid at closing, out of the sale proceeds. Nothing out of pocket.

My brothers and sisters cannot agree.

A house is impossible to split. Money is easy. A clean cash sale turns the thing nobody can divide into something everyone can, at a number and a date everybody sees in writing at the same time. We have walked a lot of families through exactly this.

I live out of state and it is full of her things.

Leave every bit of it. Furniture, clothes, paperwork, the garage, all of it. Take what matters to you and walk away from the rest. We handle the cleanout. The entire sale can be done by phone, email and remote notary. You never have to fly down.

Straight answers

Questions people actually ask

How fast can you really close?

Seven days is realistic when title is clean and you are ready. Fourteen days is typical. If there is a probate, a lien, an open permit, or a title issue, it takes longer, and we will tell you that honestly up front instead of promising a week and missing it. You can also go slower on purpose. If you need 60 days to find your next place, that is completely fine.

Are there any fees, commissions, or costs to me?

No. Zero commissions, zero fees, and we pay the standard closing costs. The number on your offer is the number that gets wired to you, minus only what you already owe on the property, like a mortgage payoff, back taxes, or liens. There is nothing buried in the paperwork.

Will you lowball me?

We are not going to pretend a cash offer is retail. It is not, and we say so right on this page. What we will not do is waste your time with a number that does not make sense, and we will not quote you high to get the contract and then grind you down after inspection. If we cannot pay enough to actually help you, we will say so on the first call and point you somewhere better.

Do I need to clean it out or fix anything?

Nothing. Not one thing. Do not paint, do not repair, do not haul anything to the dump, do not even sweep. Take the belongings that matter to you and leave the rest exactly where it is. We handle the cleanout, and it is already priced into the offer.

Can I sell a house that is still in probate?

In most cases, yes. Once the estate is opened with the Florida circuit court and a personal representative has been appointed, you can accept an offer and sign a contract. The sale funds once the personal representative has the court's authority to convey. We do this constantly, and we will connect you with a probate attorney at no upfront cost, paid at closing out of the proceeds.

What if the heirs do not agree on selling?

It is one of the most common reasons these homes sit empty for years. A house cannot be split; cash can. Everyone sees the same number and the same closing date in writing at the same time, which takes the argument off the table. In a formal administration, the personal representative generally has authority to sell with court approval. We can walk you through what applies to your estate.

How do you come up with the offer?

We look at what the house would realistically sell for once it is fixed up, what it will cost to get it there, and what it costs us to carry and resell it. Then we work backward to a number that is fair to you and still works for us. We will walk you through every piece of it on the phone and show you the comps we used, so you can check our math instead of taking our word for it.
